Setup (stored locally in this browser only)

Fully hands-free: after Start, just speak. A pause of ~1.4 seconds ends your turn; she replies out loud; the mic re-opens when she finishes. Needs Chrome. Hosted on GitHub Pages the mic just works; as a local file, serve it with python3 -m http.server. Keys never leave your browser; calls go directly to Anthropic and ElevenLabs. Without an ElevenLabs key the browser voice is used.

What gets measured from your voice

Each of your turns is recorded (replayable below your bubble, so you hear your own tone) and measured locally: speaking rate (wpm), filler words, long pauses, and loudness steadiness. Those numbers go to the coach with the transcript. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except the text to Claude and her lines to ElevenLabs.
