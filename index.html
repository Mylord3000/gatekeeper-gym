What gets measured from your voice

Each of your turns is recorded (replayable below your bubble, so you hear your own tone) and measured locally: speaking rate (wpm), filler words, long pauses, and loudness steadiness. Those numbers go to the coach with the transcript. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except the text to Claude and the gatekeeper's lines to ElevenLabs.
