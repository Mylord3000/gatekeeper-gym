What gets measured

Your turns are recorded (replayable) and measured locally: wpm, filler words, long pauses, loudness steadiness, and turn length (monologues over ~45 s get flagged). Their hidden engagement score after each reply drives the bar and goes to the coach as a trajectory, so the report can point at the exact moment you lost or won them.
